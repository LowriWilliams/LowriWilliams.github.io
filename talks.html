Unsafe Intelligence? Exploring Cybersecurity and Trust in AI-Powered Transportation

Event
Cybearly Forecasting 2027, international cybersecurity awareness conference
Venue
Aurum, Pescara, Italy
Date
October 2026
Format
Invited talk

Motorways, traffic management and vehicles increasingly depend on AI to make decisions in real time. That brings real gains in safety and efficiency, but it also creates new ways for things to go wrong, whether through deliberate attack or through models behaving in ways nobody anticipated.

This talk looks at where AI is already embedded in transport infrastructure, how those systems can be manipulated or fail, and what it takes for the public, operators and regulators to trust them. It draws on recent research into intelligent motorways and on ongoing work exploring AI-induced safety risks on the UK’s strategic road network.

Key themes

  • AI in intelligent transport
  • Adversarial machine learning
  • Safety-critical systems
  • Trust and assurance
